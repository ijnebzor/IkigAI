## Ring 1 — Identity

Studying AI as if it were a culture worth understanding rather than a tool to wield. ## The four-circle test (as a filter, not architecture)

For any project, which circle is missing — and what structural decision closes the gap?

- **Missing Paid →** commercialise it, house it in the NFP, or accept it lives in Passion/Mission and stop trying to drag it elsewhere
- **Missing World needs →** it's a hobby, and that's a complete answer (Knife Fight, ijneb studios, HomeVibes — these don't need to be more)
- **Missing Good at →** don't start it
- **Missing Love →** it's the day job; structure it to import as much of the other three rings as possible

2. **The unifying thread is sovereignty.** Five layers. Same idea at different scales.
3. **The NFP corp is the structural answer.** ERPNext over Odoo, applied to the funding problem.

## Current vs aspirational

### Current (where my time and attention actually go)

- **Heavy:** Phish Focus product work (paid). Digiquarium research and infra (mission). IkigAI build (mission tilting Ikigai). Music (passion).
- **Medium:** Podcast, blog, speaking. ijneb.dev. The active project portfolio (PrepAIred, AABB, RenovAIter, TrAIdie, wAIter).
- **Light:** bAIj (the productisation candidate). NFP corp setup follow-up. Sustained writing for the AIthropology paper.

### Aspirational (12 months)

- **bAIj productised** with NFP-housed Tier 1 work continuing in parallel
- **AIthropology paper published**
- **The Digiquarium operating semi-autonomously** with daemons doing 95% of operations
- **IkigAI app at S5** — friend-ready, bespoke as generated, not engineered
- **Speaking / podcast / brand income** ≥ 10% of total
- **Less drift between stated priorities and actual time spent** — IkigAI's drift detection should show this getting tighter

### The gap

The Mission problem. Most of what I do has no Paid path because I haven't structured one. bAIj closes that for one project; the NFP closes it for the rest. Both need attention they aren't getting.

The drift surface in IkigAI's morning brief is supposed to make this visible. If I'm saying "bAIj is the priority" but spent 14 hours on PH2 this week, the brief should show that — and let me decide if that's correct alignment or genuine drift.
