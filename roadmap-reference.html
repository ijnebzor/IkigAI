Schema v0.2

A self-owned, idea-to-action engine grounded in everything you know. You drop ideas in. The machine canonicalises, links, surfaces, coaches, and schedules. You make and iterate. Better every day.

00.1 Purpose

IkigAI runs on your always-on home device, ingests anything you send it from anywhere, and helps you turn ideas into action against the life you're actually trying to build.

It owns your knowledge. It explains everything you've ever sent it. It tells you how you got to a thought, what you might be missing, and what to do next. It knows your calendar, your projects, and your Ikigai gaps — so when you drop an idea in, it returns a phased plan and offers you the time to start.

00.2 Core principles

  • You own everything. Markdown vault on your NUC. Git is the memory trail. No vendor lock-in.
  • Capture is frictionless. Any device, any medium, share-sheet to inbox. Zero thought between brain fart and saved.
  • Canonicalisation is sacred. The dedup engine is what makes the system functional day one. Multi-key matching converges every messy capture on one record per concept.
  • Ikigai regions are the lens. Every page carries every region it touches as a flat, explicit list. A single capture compounds across passion, mission, vocation, profession, centre.
  • Retrieval has gears. Links / synthesis / debaiser. Choose per query. The system remembers per context.
  • The daily brief is the hook. Every day starts with "what do you want to work on today?" — across active projects, promoted ideas, and recent calendar reality.
  • Productionised idea flows. Idea in → phased plan out → calendar slots offered → work begins.
  • Local-first inference, cloud where it earns it. Ollama for ingest and drafts; Claude for synthesis polish and Debaiser-on-self.
  • Phase 0 is a one-time bridge. Brain dump from messaging apps and tab piles, copy-paste only, never again.
  • Bespoke per user, universal under the hood. Core (schema, prompts, daemons) is shared. Me (vocabulary, vault, calendar links) is yours. Friend's version is a fork point, not a refactor.

00.3 What's different from ABENAKI

Why this isn't ABENAKI v2

ABENAKI was the December 2025 plan. The OSS landscape caught up: Karpathy's LLM Wiki pattern, the rohitg00 v2 lifecycle gist, llm-wiki-agent, swarmvault, LightRAG. Your own production engines — canonicalisation, gargAIntuan, context-injection synthesis — proved out in council-document work.

IkigAI keeps ABENAKI's cognitive loop but rebuilds the substrate with patterns proven since: explicit Ikigai region tagging instead of derived booleans, retrieval-score evolution from observed use instead of decay alone, three-gear retrieval instead of one synthesis output, productionised idea flow with calendar offers instead of just backlog extraction. Same intent. Sharper foundations. Bespoke from day one.

ENGINE 01
Canonicalisation & dedup

Multi-key matching: title fuzzy, version, content hash, semantic similarity. Confidence-scored merging across source systems.

Already productionised in multi-source data-intelligence work. Retargeted here at messy personal captures arriving from chrome dumps, voice memos, chat-pastes.

ENGINE 02
gargAIntuan orchestrator

Parallel Claude-agent dispatch, job queue with streamed execution logs, tier-gated methodology framework, full messaging audit trail.

Already productionised for local-government compliance evidence. Retargeted here as the orchestration spine for parallel classification + linkage + reflection at ingest.

ENGINE 03
Context-injection synthesis

Claude with context-injection patterns for role-aware output generation.

Already productionised in B2B personalisation. Retargeted here to produce audience-specific Gear 2 synthesis, Gear 3 Debaiser panels, and morning brief copy in the user's chosen voice.

Roughly 80% of the technical lift for IkigAI is already written. The fee structure on this build reflects retargeting effort and integration work, not three months of from-scratch engineering. That's why the timeline compresses from quarters to weeks.

The Ikigai lens — flat, explicit, compounding

Every captured page carries every region it touches as a flat list. A single contribution lights up every region simultaneously. Click any region in the viewer; see everything that ever contributed.

03.2 The flat-list rule

A page touching love and world_needs writes both the axes and the implied intersection in ikigai_regions:

ikigai_regions: [love, world_needs, mission]

The intersection is written explicitly even though it's derivable. Because:

  • Filtering becomes one operation, not a join
  • wiki/ikigai/mission.md rolls up everything tagged mission directly
  • A single contribution compounds across every region it touches
  • Click any region in the viewer → see everything that contributed (including pages already counted under other regions)

03.3 The centre case

ikigai_regions: [love, good_at, world_needs, paid_for, passion, mission, profession, vocation, centre]

This is rare and worth flagging. The centre.md rollup page should never have many entries. If it does, either the tagging is too generous or you've actually found your Ikigai. Lint will warn either way.

Three retrieval gears — the AI opt-in slider

Same query, three depths. Gear inferred from phrasing; user can override. Local model handles 1 and drafts 2. Cloud model handles polish of 2 and all of 3.

GEAR 01 · LINKS
Pure retrieval

Vector + graph + BM25 search. Returns ranked list of pages with summaries. No synthesis.

Local only. Free, instant.

"Just give me what I sent myself about X."

GEAR 02 · SYNTHESIS
Five-section coach output

Answer · trail · action · blind spot · biases. Local model drafts; cloud model polishes if asked.

Default gear.

"What do I think about X, and how did I get there?"

GEAR 03 · DEBAISER
Contrarian panel on yourself

Five analyst voices on your own corpus: confirmer, contrarian, source-skeptic, bias-watcher, blind-spot mapper. Synthesis pass.

Cloud required. Slower, deliberate.

"Push back on me about X."

Trail: how I got here from your corpus
- <claim> — [[source-id]] (confidence 0.X, reinforced N times)
- <claim> — [[concept-id]]

Action: what you can do with this
- <concrete next move, framed against your active projects>

Blind spot: what you might not be considering
- <a contrarian source from your own corpus, or a wiki gap>

Biases: what may have shaped this thinking
- <flagged on the chain — recency, confirmation, sunk-cost>

S1.1 Goal

Confirm the schema works on a real example, not a smoke test. If anything feels wrong here, fix it now — fixing later means migrating every page in the vault.

S1.2 The round trip

  1. Pick one real source. Article you'd normally tab-stash, voice memo you've been meaning to action, copy-paste from a chat, project idea you've been circling. Don't pick something fake.
  2. Drop it in inbox/unsorted/ as a markdown file. No frontmatter needed — the pipeline writes it.
  3. Run /understand manually through Claude. Paste the prompt + your CLAUDE.md + ikigai.md + the source. Inspect the output.
  4. Save the resulting page to wiki/sources/<id>.md.
  5. Run /retrieve at all three gears against the corpus-of-one. Validate the output shape for each gear.
  6. Adjust the schema if needed. Bump schema_hash if you change anything structural.

It runs whether you're at the keyboard or not. Capture in, action out, outcomes observed, brain learns.

1.  You capture a thought, link, voice memo, quote — from anywhere.
2.  Inbox-watcher picks it up on the NUC.
3.  Canonicalisation engine merges with existing records or creates new.
4.  gargAIntuan runs classification, linkage, reflection in parallel.
5.  Page lands in vault with Ikigai regions, summary, provenance, linkages.
6.  Linkage-scout walks the graph that night, finds non-obvious connections.
7.  Brief-generator builds tomorrow's options across your projects.
8.  Morning: you read the brief on your phone or web viewer.
9.  You pick what to work on. Calendar event lands in Google Cal.
10. You retrieve at the gear that fits — links, synthesis, or coach.
11. You act on what landed.
12. Application capture closes the ADDIE loop.
13. Outcome observed; retrieval_score and context_modifiers update.
14. Tomorrow's brief is shaped by today's reality.
The compounding property

Every step feeds the next iteration. Capture compounds (canonicalisation merges duplicates into one record that gets stronger). Classification compounds (more sources tagged with a region make that region's rollup richer). Retrieval compounds (use shapes rank, rank shapes use). Phased plans compound (each shipped phase unlocks the next without re-planning). The system gets smarter as a function of being used, not as a function of being trained.
