What it does
// five layers, one engine
01 / CAPTURE

Anywhere, any medium

Share-sheet, voice memo, hotkey, paste, email-to-self. Zero thought between brain fart and saved.

02 / UNDERSTAND

Canonicalise & classify

Multi-key dedup. Ikigai region tagging. Entities and concepts extracted. Linkages proposed against everything you've ever sent.

03 / RETAIN

Markdown vault, yours forever

Plain text on your home device. Git is the memory trail. Vector and graph indexes derive from it. No vendor lock-in.

04 / SURFACE

Three retrieval gears

Links only. Synthesis with provenance. Or the Debaiser panel — five analyst voices on your own corpus, pushing back.

05 / GROW

Productionised idea flows

Idea in. Phased plan out. Calendar slots offered against your real availability. Make and iterate.

∞ / COMPOUND

Better every day

Watches what you actually use. Surfaces non-obvious linkages overnight. Learns the shape of your mind without you grading it.

The principles
// what stays constant across every stage

Sovereignty

Local-first. Self-owned. Your data never leaves your device unless you explicitly send it.

Frictionless

If capture isn't zero-friction, the brain dies. Any device, any medium, anywhere.

Bespoke

Every IkigAI is different. Every brain is different. Universal under the hood; yours on the surface.

Compounding

One capture lights up every Ikigai region it touches. Retrieval improves with use. Phases unlock the next.
