ikigAI

A tool I built for myself, to make myself better.
You can have it too.

The industry wants to erode your critical thinking and turn you into a robot. This is the opposite. You remain the thinker. The system makes the compounding visible.

you give it what you know.
you give it where you're going.
the framework overlays.
compounding becomes visible.
you act on what was already there.
Open The spec

It's a coach in the way that it points things out to you.

That's all it's doing. Taking the information you gave it, with the goals you provided, overlaying the four-axis Ikigai framework, and giving you frictionless ability to complete your goals in a compounded and recursive way.

Do one thing. It might impact two regions of your Ikigai you didn't realise. The system points that out. You decide what to do with it.

You remain the thinker. The system stays observational. No directives. No advice. No "you should." Just visibility on overlap.

// WHAT WORKS RIGHT NOW

Today (browser-only): Open the app. Bulk-paste 50 Chrome tabs into Capture. Each gets tagged with the regions it touches. The Lens lights up — Venn, bars, markdown, JSON, or spoken. Your stones, your vocabulary, your view modes. Stored in localStorage and Drive-syncable.

Saturday (NUC live): Daemons take over. THE WATCHER auto-ingests everything you drop. THE SCOUT finds linkages overnight. THE HERALD writes a daily brief. THE DEBAISER runs contrarian panels. The PWA becomes the cockpit; the NUC becomes the brain.

What today builds toward: Every capture you make right now is seed data the daemons will find connections in on Saturday. Not wasted effort. Front-loading.
